Frequently asked questions

Booking & Availability

You can book directly via our website location-chalet-jostedalen.com, via Chalet-Montagne.com or through Airbnb. For a direct booking, contact us via the website form, SMS or WhatsApp. We will send you a rental agreement to sign and return along with a 30% deposit, preferably by bank transfer or by cheque.

During high season (winter and summer), rentals run Saturday to Saturday. Exceptions may be possible depending on calendar availability — feel free to contact us to discuss. Off-season weekend rentals are available.

Yes, this is possible in spring or autumn, with a 2-night minimum. Not during high season (winter and summer), when rentals run Saturday to Saturday.

Yes, direct booking rates are more advantageous as there are no platform fees.

Chalet Jostedalen is a 240 m² triplex with 6 enclosed bedrooms (2 of which are under sloped roofs). It comfortably accommodates groups of 12 to 14 people (adults and children). It also has 2 baby cots (travel cots).

The chalet has 6 enclosed bedrooms, 2 of which are located under sloped roofs. These are full bedrooms, not mezzanines.

The chalet has 2 living rooms. A TV/children’s lounge on the first floor and a large living room with views under the rafters.

Please contact us directly to discuss the possibility of bringing your pet. Each request is considered on a case-by-case basis. Please mention it when making your booking request.

Yes, the chalet is available all year round. In winter, you can enjoy the Sybelles ski area just 200 m away. In summer, the mountains offer numerous hiking and outdoor activity options.

High season weeks are offered first to our direct guests. Only remaining weeks are listed on Airbnb. Booking directly gives you access to a wider choice of dates.

We recommend booking 8 to 14 months in advance for school holidays in winter (Christmas, February) and summer (July–August). Contact us as soon as your dates are confirmed.

Rates & Payment

The rate includes the rental price (varies by season) and cleaning fees. The tourist tax (~€1/adult/night) is payable online before your arrival via a secure payment link. Linen is optional.

A 30% deposit is required upon signing the contract (preferably by bank transfer or cheque). The balance is due 1 month before your arrival as per the rental agreement terms. Bank details are provided at the end of the contract.

Yes, a €1,000 security deposit is required. It must be paid separately from the balance by bank transfer or cheque before your arrival.

The security deposit is returned by bank transfer after your departure and inspection of the chalet’s condition.

Full refund if cancelled 60 days before your arrival.

No, we do not offer special rates for ski passes in the Sybelles area. You can purchase them directly from the resort or online on the Sybelles website.

Sheets, Towels & Linen

The chalet is rented without sheets or towels. You can bring your own or rent linen kits (sheets + towels) on site. Please let us know in advance if you would like this option.

Yes, the chalet is equipped with single duvets.

There are 2 travel cots with mattresses included in the rental. However, sheets and duvets for baby cots are not provided. We recommend bringing your own linen for little ones.

Yes, we offer the “beds made on arrival” option. Specify this when making your booking.

Yes, we can offer a rate including linen kits for your entire group. Contact us specifying the number of guests for a personalised quote.

Arrival & Departure

Check-in is from 5 pm and check-out is before 10 am.

Someone will be available to welcome you on site or by phone. Their contact details will be provided before your arrival. You will also receive all access instructions (GPS address, entry code, WiFi, etc.).

Chalet Jostedalen is located at the top of Saint-Sorlin d’Arves, at the place called Pierre Aiguë, in the heart of the Sybelles ski area. A GPS link will be sent before your arrival to facilitate access.

The chalet should be left in a reasonably clean state: dishes done, bins emptied, duvets folded. A professional clean is carried out after each stay.

Insurance & Liability

Yes, the chalet is insured by the owner. However, each tenant must have their own personal liability insurance (included in most home insurance policies).

Amenities & Comfort

Yes, the chalet has a private spa/jacuzzi. Safety rules must be followed: never leave children unsupervised, do not stay more than 20 minutes if the temperature exceeds 37.5°C, and always replace the spa cover after each use.

Yes, the chalet has WiFi. Login details will be provided before your arrival.

Yes, the chalet has AirPlay connected speakers in the upper living room.

The kitchen is fully equipped including: an automatic bean-to-cup coffee machine (plus a filter coffee maker), 2 fondue sets and 2 raclette machines — perfect for convivial mountain evenings!

Yes, the chalet has a garage that can accommodate one car. Important: the garage door must be securely closed at night to prevent any risk of pipes freezing.

Yes, the chalet has 2 travel cots with mattresses, included in the rental.

Yes, the chalet is equipped with a washing machine and a tumble dryer for guests' use.

Yes, the chalet has a television in the lower living room.

No, the chalet does not have a fireplace or wood-burning stove. Heating is mainly underfloor, providing even and comfortable warmth throughout the chalet.

Basic cleaning supplies are provided for the start of your stay (washing-up liquid, sponge, tea towels, toilet paper). For a one-week stay, we recommend planning to restock.

Parking

You can park one car in the garage and one or two cars in front of the chalet. It is important to keep the area in front of the chalet clear for traffic. For additional vehicles, spaces are available further up in the village or before the large bend before the chalet.

Yes, the chalet has a 22 kW charging station. It is available to guests during off-peak hours.

House Rules

No, smoking is strictly prohibited inside the chalet, even with windows open.

Heating is underfloor. We ask that you do not leave windows and doors open for extended periods, as extreme cold cools the chalet quickly and underfloor heating has significant inertia to regain the initial temperature.

For safety reasons, balcony access is not permitted.

Never leave children unsupervised in the spa. Do not stay more than 20 minutes if the temperature exceeds 37.5°C (risk of hyperthermia). Always replace the spa cover after use and reset the heating to 37.5°C.

Tourist Tax

The tourist tax is approximately €1/adult/night (children are exempt). A secure payment link will be sent before your arrival. Simply adjust the number of guests and proceed with online payment before your stay.

Skiing & Slope Access

Yes, the slopes are approximately 300 m on foot from the chalet. A free shuttle also runs 30 m from the chalet for those who prefer not to carry their skis.

The nearest chairlift is Les Choseaux. You can also take the free shuttle (stop 30 m from the chalet) to reach the ski lifts.

Everything is easily accessible. The ski lifts are approximately 300 m on foot and the ESF is located at the ski lift area. The free shuttle (stop 30 m from the chalet) also takes you there.

Yes, it is a family-friendly resort with good snow coverage. It is connected to the Sybelles area which offers over 300 km of slopes.

The chalet is located at the top of Saint-Sorlin d’Arves, at Pierre Aiguë. The village has two centres: the village centre at the bottom and a snow front at the top closer to the chalet. The free shuttle (stop 30 m away) serves the entire resort.

Yes, a Skiset “CyberSki” shop is right next to the chalet. You can rent your ski equipment directly on site, without taking the car. Chalet guests may receive a 20 to 25 % discount depending on the period. Contact Pascale:

The Chalet

The chalet is semi-detached on the sides only. It is not an apartment on a floor: it is an independent 240 m² triplex with its own entrance, garage and garden.

Practical & Access

Yes, in winter, it is strongly recommended to equip your vehicle with snow tyres or to have chains in your boot. Under French mountain law, this is mandatory from November to March for driving in mountain areas. The access road to Saint-Sorlin d'Arves can be snowy, especially in the evening.

Saint-Sorlin d'Arves has essential shops: bakery, butcher, grocery, pharmacy, restaurants. The nearest shops are at the top of the village, approximately 200 m from the chalet.

Standard times are: check-in from 5 pm, check-out before 10 am. These slots are necessary to allow cleaning and preparation of the chalet between stays. If you have a special need, contact us and we will do our best to accommodate you subject to availability.

Yes, the Sherpa in the village centre offers a delivery service. Contact them directly to arrange your delivery.

Children & Safety

The chalet has 2 travel cots with mattresses and high chairs. However, as with any multi-level mountain chalet (triplex), it has stairs. We recommend staying vigilant with toddlers, especially near the stairs and the spa. A stair gate is available for the upper living room.

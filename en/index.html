Frequently asked questions

The chalet is located at the top of the village of Saint-Sorlin d'Arves (1600 m). Access via the A43 motorway, exit Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne, then D926. Private garage for 1 car, free parking nearby.

Check-in from 5 pm, check-out before 10 am. Secure key box for self check-in.

Duvets are provided (1 per bed). Sheets and towels are not provided. Rental available as an option.

Les Sybelles ski area (310 km of runs, 4th largest in France) is 200 m from the chalet. Free shuttle at 30 m. Ski passes available at the resort ticket offices.

Yes, pets are accepted in the chalet on request.

Hiking, climbing, horse riding, via ferrata, paragliding. Supervised lake at 200 m. Garden with barbecue.
